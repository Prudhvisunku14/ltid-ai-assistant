# Meridian Logistics Information Security Policy

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.
Passwords should be rotated every 180 days.

## Data in Transit
All external connections must use TLS 1.3.

## Audit Logging
Access to customer data shall be logged and logs kept for 24 months.

## Vendor Access
Third-party vendors must be approved by the security team before accessing production systems.
