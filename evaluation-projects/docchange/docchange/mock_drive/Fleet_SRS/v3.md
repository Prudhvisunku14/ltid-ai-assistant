# Meridian Logistics - FleetTrack Software Requirements Specification
Owner: Product Management. Status: Draft 3 (approved).

## 1. Performance
FT-101: The system shall display vehicle positions within 2 seconds of receipt from the device.
FT-102: The platform shall support 1200 concurrent dashboard users.
FT-103: Monthly availability shall be at least 99.9%.

## 2. Authentication and Access Control
FT-201: Users shall authenticate through the corporate single sign-on (OAuth 2.0).
FT-202: Administrators shall use multi-factor authentication.

## 3. Alerts
FT-301: The system shall send an e-mail alert when a vehicle leaves its geofence.
FT-302: Alerts may be acknowledged by dispatchers.
FT-303: The system should send an SMS alert for critical geofence breaches.

## 4. Reporting
FT-401: The system shall export trip reports as CSV and PDF.

## 5. Platforms
FT-501: The web dashboard shall support Chrome, Firefox and Safari.
FT-502: A native Android driver app shall be delivered by Q3.
