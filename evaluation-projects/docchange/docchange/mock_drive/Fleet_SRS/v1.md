# Meridian Logistics - FleetTrack Software Requirements Specification
Owner: Product Management. Status: Draft 1.

## 1. Performance
FT-101: The system shall display vehicle positions within 5 seconds of receipt from the device.
FT-102: The platform shall support 500 concurrent dashboard users.
FT-103: Monthly availability shall be at least 99.5%.

## 2. Authentication
FT-201: Users shall authenticate with username and password.
FT-202: Administrators should use multi-factor authentication.

## 3. Alerts
FT-301: The system shall send an e-mail alert when a vehicle leaves its geofence.
FT-302: Alerts may be acknowledged by dispatchers.

## 4. Reporting
FT-401: The system shall export trip reports as CSV.
FT-402: Reports shall cover the previous 12 months.

## 5. Platforms
FT-501: The web dashboard shall support Chrome and Firefox.
