NORTHSTAR SERVICES — EXPENSE POLICY — VERSION 2 (effective 2026-10-01; supersedes version 1)  
All claims must be submitted within 10 calendar days after an expense occurs.  
Receipts are required for any individual expense of JPY 2,000 or more.  
Domestic travel expenses require manager approval before the trip. International travel requires finance and manager approval.  
The reimbursement cap for one domestic hotel night is JPY 15,000, excluding applicable taxes.  
For expense-related questions, cite this document and its effective date.
